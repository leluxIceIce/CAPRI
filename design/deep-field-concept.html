02The encoding law

Colour is never decoration.

Hue = which variable or state · luminance = value · saturation & alpha = confidence and density · motion = flux. The background is always the darkest thing, so luminous data reads at maximum contrast — the lesson of every reference plate: beautiful because rigorous.

03Data as living systems

Every variable becomes a thing you can feel.

Each mapping is tied to a computation CAPRI already performs. Nothing is invented; the science is spatialised.

Fronts become terrain · was: gradient overlay

The nine spatial-structure descriptors rise into ridges and rifts. Where algorithms disagree, or gradients spike, a luminous seam cuts the field — a front you see from across the scene.

spatialTensor · Moran's I

Regimes become rooms · was: k-means table

Seeded k-means clusters render as translucent membranes draping the field. The dominant regime is the room you stand in; a mixing frontier is two coloured fogs bleeding together.

regime posteriors · convex hulls

Uncertainty becomes fog · was: confidence plane

Per-cell confidence becomes the clarity of the water itself. Low confidence thickens into haze — you literally cannot see clearly where the data is uncertain. Epistemics, in the optics.

aerosol confidence [0,1]

Relationships become a constellation · was: corr. heatmap

Channels are nodes; correlations are luminous edges — brightness by |r|, hue by sign. Tightly-coupled channels visibly cluster. The Pearson-r grid stays one gesture away.

relationshipTensor · Pearson r

Risk becomes weather · was: alert badge

The bloom detector tints the whole scene's atmosphere: clear for green, a gathering amber haze, a charged crimson storm-light. You feel the alert level on arrival, before reading a word.

GAIA bloom · GREEN/AMBER/RED

Novelty becomes a beacon · was: p-value row

The Mahalanobis novelty test lights a ranging reticle on the region it caught, its p-value in mono beside it. A genuine "the sensor found something" moment, not a table cell.

Mahalanobis · p-value

05What survives · what dissolves

Preserve every information-carrier. Dissolve every pure frame.

The test for each element: does it carry information, or merely frame it? Carriers are kept — often spatialised. Frames become the instrument itself.

Preserved

  • The 3D layered stack — CAPRI's soul; elevated to the environment
  • Toggle · opacity · reorder · custom colour per layer, as fluid in-scene controls
  • Scientific colormaps & legends — the honest key, always reachable
  • The auto-generated narrator — promoted to the bridge from feeling to understanding
  • Raw-numbers escape hatches — CSV rows, exact stats, actual-vs-predicted, colorbars
  • Client-side · seeded · no telemetry, and the WebGL context-loss recovery — every line

Dissolved

  • The three-column dashboard shell → one instrument-space with summonable trays
  • Stat cards as the primary readout → read-out gutter + ambient hover + narrator
  • Analysis modals → rooms you fly to and trays that slide, never freezing the app
  • Generic SaaS chrome → instrument scaffolding and margin metadata
  • Abrupt frame updates → advected, continuous transitions
  • Correlation heatmap-first → constellation-first, heatmap on demand
100% PRESERVED

Every channel, analysis, overlay, source, and export in the current app is re-housed intact. The redesign is a re-housing of a scientific engine, not a re-scoping of it.
